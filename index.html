About this site

ZUSE LAB is a private project studio run by Ben Schaarschmidt. It brings together his own ventures, brands and publishing projects under one roof.

The studio is run agent-first: day-to-day work is carried out by AI-supported roles under human direction. They run on OpenClaw, an open-source agent framework, using large language models from Anthropic, OpenAI and others. Decisions, contracts and publications remain with the owner.

The roles shown in the team section are AI-supported roles, not employees. Names are fictitious and the portraits are AI-generated. Quotes are attributed to their original authors.
